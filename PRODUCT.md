# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, static export) chosen by the client. GSAP + ScrollTrigger and
Lenis for scroll-driven motion. Images pre-encoded to AVIF with WebP fallback.

## Users

Prospective clients of an Azerbaijani interior studio: homeowners furnishing a new
apartment or villa, and business owners fitting out an office. Most arrive from
Instagram (`@buondesign.az`) on a phone, often in the evening, deciding whether this
studio's taste matches theirs before they message.

## Product Purpose

The site is the studio's showroom. Success: a visitor feels what it is like to live
inside a Buon Design interior, understands the three disciplines (interior,
furniture, architecture), and messages the studio.

## Positioning

Buon Design designs, makes the furniture for, and architecturally plans the same
space — one studio from walls to sofa. Its renders show rooms lived in, in warm
low sun, not empty showrooms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: Azerbaijani (primary), Russian, English. Fonts must cover ə Ə ğ ı İ ş ç ö ü and Cyrillic.
- Contact: Instagram only for now. Phone, email and address are undecided — do not invent them.
- Must load fast on mobile: short loader, AVIF imagery, minimal JS.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: BD monogram (ink "B", champagne-gold crescent "D"), wordmark BUON DESIGN,
  line INTERIOR • FURNITURE • ARCHITECTURE, signature script "Live Beautifully".
- Taglines in use: "Live Beautifully", "Luxury is Status".
- Client brief: luxury, modern, niche; no generic AI gradients. Scroll should let the
  visitor walk into a concept apartment. Loader animates the logo and a building.

## Evidence on Hand

- Concept apartment (neoclassical): living room, sofa, dining, reading nook — frames
  from the studio's reel, `assets-src/apt-*.jpg`.
- Executive office project: four photos, `assets-src/office-*.jpg`.
- "2027 Premium Kitchen Tones" carousel: six named finishes, `assets-src/kitchen-*.jpg`.
- No testimonials, client names, project counts, years, prices or awards exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Let the rooms lead; interface recedes.
2. Show life in the space, not just objects.
3. Every claim on the page is something the studio actually showed.
4. Calm, expensive motion: slow where the visitor explores, instant where they act.
